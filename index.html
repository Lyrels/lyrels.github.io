Flitdo

Capture it. Get it done.随手记下，轻松完成。

A minimal to-do app for Android with a floating quick-add bubble. Jot a task down from any app, let Flitdo read the date and time from what you typed, and get reminded right on time. 一款简洁的 Android 待办应用，带有悬浮快速添加气泡。在任何应用里随手记下任务，Flitdo 会从你输入的文字中识别日期和时间，并准时提醒你。

  • Quick add from anywhere with the floating bubble悬浮气泡，在任何界面快速添加
  • Understands dates and times in English and Chinese, offline离线识别中英文日期和时间
  • No account, no ads, no tracking — tasks stay on your phone无需账号、无广告、无追踪，任务只存在你的手机上

How we work我们的原则

Three things we don’t compromise on我们坚持的三件事

Focused专注

Each app does one job well. Fewer screens, fewer settings you never touch, and nothing that gets in your way.每个应用只把一件事做好。更少的页面，没有用不上的设置，不打扰你。

Private by default默认保护隐私

No accounts, ads or analytics unless a feature truly needs them. What you write stays on your device.除非功能确实需要，否则不做账号、广告和统计分析。你写下的内容只留在你的设备上。

Made for everyone为每个人设计

English and Chinese, light and dark, screen readers, phones, tablets and foldables — details are part of the product.中文与英文、浅色与深色、读屏、手机、平板与折叠屏——细节本身就是产品的一部分。
