Features功能

Everything a to-do app needs. Nothing it doesn’t.待办应用该有的都有，不该有的一样没有。

Flitdo is built around one idea: writing a task down should take a second, and remembering it should be the app’s job.Flitdo 围绕一个想法打造：记下一件事只该花一秒钟，记住它是应用的事。

Understands what you type读懂你写的时间

Write naturally in English or Chinese. Flitdo recognises dates and times right on your phone — no AI service, no server — and shows what it understood before saving. Ambiguous input is never guessed, and you can teach it your own phrasing with templates. 用中文或英文自然地写。Flitdo 直接在手机上识别日期和时间——不用 AI 服务，也不连服务器——保存前会先告诉你它识别到了什么。有歧义的输入绝不瞎猜，你还可以用模板教它认识你自己的说法。

Floating quick-add bubble悬浮快速添加气泡

An optional + bubble floats over other apps. Tap, type, done — without leaving what you’re doing. When idle it slides into the screen edge, out of your way.可选的 + 气泡悬浮在其他应用之上。点一下、输入、完成，不必离开手头的事。闲置时会滑进屏幕边缘，不挡视线。

Reminders you can rely on可靠的提醒

Local notifications with Done and Snooze that survive restarts, updates and time-zone changes. Optionally keep reminding until handled, with quiet hours and your own sound.带“完成”和“稍后提醒”的本地通知，重启、更新、切换时区后依然有效。还可以选择持续提醒直到处理，支持免打扰时段和自定义提示音。

Repeating tasks重复任务

Every day, every weekday, chosen days of the week, every few days, monthly or yearly. There is always exactly one open occurrence — missed ones never pile up.每天、每个工作日、每周指定几天、每隔几天、每月或每年。始终只有一个待办的实例，错过的不会越堆越多。

Make it yours按你的喜好定制

Pick the bubble’s icon (or use your own image), size, theme, colours and a gentle reminder animation. Colours that would hurt readability are corrected automatically.自选气泡图标（也可以用自己的图片）、大小、主题、颜色和柔和的提醒动画。影响可读性的配色会被自动修正。

Plan by date按日期规划

Today, Upcoming and Completed at a glance, plus a month calendar that marks what is due on each day. Status is always shown with an icon and a word as well as a colour, never colour alone.“今天”“即将到来”“已完成”一目了然，月历还会标出每天的待办。状态除了颜色，还始终配有图标和文字，绝不只靠颜色区分。

Undo, always随时撤销

Deleted the wrong task — or a whole section? Bring it back within 15 seconds, even if the app was closed in between.删错了一个任务，甚至一整组？15 秒内都能恢复，即使中途关掉了应用也可以。

Privacy隐私

Your tasks stay on your phone.你的任务只留在你的手机上。

Flitdo doesn’t collect, send or share your data. It doesn’t even ask for Internet access, so there is simply no way for your tasks to leave the device through the app.Flitdo 不收集、不上传、也不分享你的数据。它甚至不申请联网权限，你的任务根本没有途径通过应用离开设备。

Read the privacy policy阅读隐私政策
  • No Internet permission不申请联网权限
  • No account or sign-up无需账号，无需注册
  • No ads, analytics or tracking无广告、无统计、无追踪
  • Smart input runs fully offline智能识别完全离线运行
Permissions权限

Only what a feature needs只申请功能必需的权限

Each permission is used for exactly one thing. Without any of them, your task list and calendar keep working.每项权限只用于一件事。即使全部不授予，任务列表和日历依然可以正常使用。

Permission权限What it’s for用途
Notifications通知Showing your reminders.显示你设置的提醒。
Alarms & reminders闹钟和提醒Delivering reminders exactly on time. Without it, Android decides when they arrive.让提醒准时送达。未授予时，由 Android 决定提醒的送达时间。
Display over other apps显示在其他应用上层The optional floating bubble only. Off by default.仅用于可选的悬浮气泡，默认关闭。
Foreground service前台服务Keeping the bubble available while you have it switched on. A notification shows while it runs and lets you turn it off.在你开启气泡期间保持其可用。运行时会显示一条通知，可以随时从通知中关闭。
Run at startup开机启动Restoring reminders (and the bubble, if enabled) after the phone restarts.手机重启后恢复提醒（以及已开启的气泡）。
